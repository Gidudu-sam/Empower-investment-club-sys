# Balance Sheet Setup — Quick Summary

**Problem:** System is asking for Financial Year, Accounting Periods, and Opening Balances before showing Balance Sheet.

**Why:** Proper accounting requires a defined time period and starting point. Without these, any figures shown would be fabricated/meaningless.

---

## What You Need to Do (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Create Financial Year (5 minutes)
**Where:** Settings → Financial Years → Create New  
**What:** Define your accounting year (e.g., "FY 2026: Jan 1 - Dec 31, 2026")  
**Who:** Admin or Treasurer

---

### Step 2: Create Accounting Periods (15 minutes)
**Where:** Accounting → Accounting Periods → Create Period  
**What:** Create 12 monthly periods (Jan 2026, Feb 2026, ..., Dec 2026)  
**Who:** Admin or Treasurer

---

### Step 3: Prepare Opening Balances (1-3 hours — Most Important)
**Where:** Accounting → Opening Balances → Prepare Batch

**What You Need:**
- Count physical cash in hand
- Check bank statement balance
- Check mobile money balance
- Calculate total member savings owed
- Calculate total loans receivable
- List any other assets/liabilities

**Process:**
1. **Gather data** (Treasurer — 1-2 hours)
2. **Enter in system** (Treasurer — 30 minutes)
3. **Review & approve** (Chairman — 15 minutes)
4. **Post** (Admin/Treasurer — 2 minutes)

**Important Rules:**
- Debits must equal Credits (Assets = Liabilities + Equity)
- Same person can't prepare AND approve (security control)
- Once posted, opening balances are locked

## Common Questions

**Q: Do we do this every year?**  
A: Yes, but subsequent years are mostly automated from prior year's closing balances.

**Q: Why can't the system calculate opening balances automatically?**  
A: Historical data includes test transactions (not real club activity). Opening balances require accountant verification.

**Q: What if opening balances don't balance?**  
A: System won't let you save until Debits = Credits. Use "Retained Earnings" account to absorb any difference.

**Q: Can we change opening balances later?**  
A: Before posting = Yes (edit freely). After posting = No (would need correcting journal entry).
